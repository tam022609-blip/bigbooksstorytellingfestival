BIG BOOKS

Storytelling Festival

Background & Rationale

Stories are vehicles for initial exploration and learning through which young minds create, analyze, and build meaning. In the Philippines, addressing early literacy challenges requires vibrant, localized, and engaging narrative tools.

The Big Books Storytelling Festival empowers Senior High School and College students across Iloilo City and Province. It equips young storytellers with the skills to craft children's digital manuscripts and transform them into physical, large-format BigBooks.

Through workshops, mentorship, performance evaluation, and community caravans, the festival fosters reading enthusiasm, community identity, and creative literacy.

General Objective

To develop young people as creative storytellers and content creators while promoting children's engagement with stories, reading, listening, imagination, and meaningful learning through original digital and physical BigBooks.

Serving Iloilo City & Provincial Communities

Who May Submit?

  • Open to all Senior High School and College students residing or studying in the City and Province of Iloilo.
  • Applicants may register individually or represent their school / institutional organization.

Initial Story Submission Checklist

Each submission must contain the following required story information:

1. Story Title
2. Theme (People, Planet, etc.)
3. Story Synopsis
4. Initial Story Draft / Narrative
5. Story Message / Purpose
6. Author Name & Institution

Story Guidelines & Content Policy

Stories must be original, culturally sensitive, and child-friendly. Key themes encouraged include kindness, friendship, inclusion, environmental preservation, climate action, volunteerism, and local heritage. Plagiarism or uncredited AI-generated storybook entries are strictly prohibited.

Selection Criteria

Evaluated by the official selection committee.

Story Concept & Creativity 25%

Originality, imagination, and strength of core narrative.

Child-Appropriateness 20%

Suitability, accessibility, and appeal to children.

Storytelling Potential 20%

Adaptability for illustrations and live performance.

Message & Relevance 20%

Values, culture, environmental or community themes.

Writing & Presentation 15%

Clarity, grammar, flow, and structural organization.

TOTAL SCORE: 100%
